# 📊 FMCG Sales & Profitability Dashboard

### 1. Project Title

📊 FMCG Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence dashboard developed using Power BI and Tableau to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, sales channels, regional performance, promotions, and product-level sales.

---

### 2. Short Description

The FMCG Sales & Profitability Dashboard is an interactive analytics solution designed to provide a comprehensive view of sales and business performance.

The dashboard analyzes revenue, profit, orders, units sold, profit margin, marketing spend, product categories, customer types, sales channels, regions, and promotional performance. Interactive filters and visualizations allow users to explore business trends and identify key performance patterns.

---

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 Power BI – Used for dashboard development, interactive reporting, and data visualization.
- 📈 Tableau – Used to build and compare interactive dashboard visualizations.
- 🧹 Data Analysis – Used to analyze sales and profitability patterns.
- 📊 Data Visualization – Used to present business performance and trends.
- 🎛️ Interactive Filters – Used for dynamic analysis across different business dimensions.

---

### 4. Data Source

The dashboard uses an FMCG sales dataset containing information related to:

- Sales and revenue
- Profit and profitability
- Product categories
- Customer types
- Sales channels
- Regional performance
- Promotions
- Product-level sales
- Orders and units sold
- Marketing spend

The dataset was analyzed and transformed to develop interactive dashboards and generate meaningful business insights.

---

### 5. Features

The dashboard focuses on the following key areas:

- 💰 Sales & Revenue Analysis
- 📈 Profitability Analysis
- 📦 Product Category Analysis
- 👥 Customer Type Analysis
- 🌍 Regional Performance Analysis
- 🛒 Sales Channel Analysis
- 🎯 Promotion Performance Analysis
- 🏆 Top 10 Product Analysis
- 📊 Order & Unit Sales Analysis
- 📢 Marketing Spend Analysis
- 🎯 KPI Cards
- 🔎 Interactive Filters and Slicers

#### Business Problem

FMCG businesses generate large volumes of sales data across products, customers, regions, sales channels, and promotional activities. Without an interactive reporting solution, it can be difficult to monitor profitability, identify high-performing products, and understand sales trends across different business segments.

#### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to provide an interactive view of FMCG sales and profitability performance, enabling users to analyze key business metrics and identify trends across products, customers, regions, channels, and promotions.

#### Key Visuals

The dashboard includes visualizations for:

- Revenue and Profit Trends
- Revenue and Profit by Product Category
- Revenue and Profit by Customer Type
- Regional Revenue and Profit
- Revenue and Profit by Sales Channel
- Revenue and Total Profit by Promotion
- Cost Breakdown
- Top 10 Products
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Profit Margin
- Marketing Spend

#### Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard helps users:

- Monitor overall revenue and profitability
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Compare B2B and B2C performance
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Compare different sales channels
- Evaluate promotional performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Monitor profit margin and marketing spend
- Explore business performance using interactive filters
- Support data-driven decision-making
